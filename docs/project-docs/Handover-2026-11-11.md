## Things to know before touching the gate

- **PR #195 was merged past a gate halt** (margin -0.071, then -0.004 after its triage rows; required +0.05). Cause: a finding accepted with a recorded fix still counts in full, and Q reviews only the first push, so it never confirms a fix. The Sponsor waived it; the dated waiver is in the `## IMP-007` entry of `implementation-log.md`. The rule is not changed (IMP-065).

Reviews on #192 to #194 used other headings, so their +0.220 margins may not include Q's findings. #195's `Critical Issues` heading matched.
